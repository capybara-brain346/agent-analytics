# Dashboard

Mode: Operate. Audience: Webzero PMs and engineers. Scope: all six dashboard sections and their evidence details. Content authority: docs/modules/dashboard/ and docs/modules/classification/. The user's three images pin the visual world and override the random direction assignment (seed 3196f89c).

## Direction contract

THESIS: An evidence console for agent traffic. Findings stay beside the records that support them. Dense useful content fills the viewport.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal ground, slightly lighter panel heads, fine neutral borders, compact system sans, tabular numbers. Lavender selection; cyan, teal, amber, and muted violet encode traffic classes. Red is reserved for denied requests and failures. No glow or decorative imagery.

STORY: Read the highest-priority tests, scan traffic, then inspect a recommendation and its exact sessions and requests. Every inferred statement and unknown outcome is marked.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 204px side rail and 52px top bar frame the content. Compact heading and filter bar sit above three ranked recommendation rows. Five metric cells lead to a wide stacked traffic chart and narrower class table. Top pages and data coverage close the page. Signature interaction: a linked finding opens an evidence detail in a right sheet while the original context stays visible. No page-load animation.

FORM: User-pinned observability console; seed 3196f89c. The candidate set considered was: trace console, request ledger, incident board, route atlas, verification register, journey notebook, and evidence desk. Pinned references determine layout and controls. The exposure-record, map, marketing, type-specimen, manual, and ticket-wallet challengers are declined because their navigation and materials weaken the specified end-user console. Kept disciplines: readable contrast, clear legend, paired proof, aligned grid, consistent sections, and persistent history.
